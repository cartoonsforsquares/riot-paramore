Riot Font

Riot!

Designed by a fan for other fans.

A free, hand-drawn fan font in three styles: Marker, Scrawl and Scribble.

Each one has capital letters, numbers and basic punctuation. Lowercase keys type the capitals too, so caps lock doesn't matter.

Aa Bb 123

Use this area to test this typeface. You can also download a png of the words you type here.

Turn your concert photos into a post card. Add a photo for the front, then flip it over to write the back. Both sides save as 6×4 inch, print-ready PNGs.

Front

Drag the photo to move it.

The caption moves, resizes and turns like a sticker.

Stickers

Tap a star to stick it on the side you're looking at. Drag it to move, pull the pink corner to resize or turn it, tap × to remove it.

MacDouble-click each .otf file, then click Install Font.
WindowsRight-click each .otf file and choose Install.
ProcreateActions › Add › Add Text, then Edit Style › Import Font.

All three styles show up under one name, “Riot”. Pick Marker, Scrawl or Scribble from the style menu. The letters are one colour, so you can make them any colour you like.

Riot is free for personal use. You can use it for all forms of fan art, posters, stickers, scrapbooks, posts, and gifts etc. Hopefully none include any commercial projects *wink* *wink*.

This is a fan made project. I am not affiliated with the band or their label, and I do not get paid for this. I simply made it for the love of the game.
